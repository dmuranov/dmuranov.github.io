Privacy policy

Last updated: 25 November 2026

Fixera connects businesses (bars, restaurants, shops, offices) with nearby maintenance professionals over WhatsApp and phone calls. This page explains what data we process, why, and your rights.

Controller

Fixera. Contact: WhatsApp or phone +34 699 15 21 03.

What we process

Businesses (clients)

  • Your WhatsApp number and profile name.
  • Business name, tax ID (NIF/CIF) and address.
  • The problem description, indicative budget and the messages you send us.
  • Which professionals called you, whether you closed a deal, your rating (1-5) and feedback.
  • Payment data, handled directly by Stripe (we never see or store your card).

Professionals

  • Public data from your Google Maps listing: trading name, phone, address, rating.
  • The outcome of our calls (accepted, declined, no answer) and your answer (keypad or voice).
  • Accepted jobs, their outcome and client ratings.
  • If you press 9 during a call, we keep your number only so we never call you again.

Why we use it

  • To check the business exists (tax-ID check and its Google Maps listing).
  • To find nearby professionals, call them and give each side the other's contact.
  • To send notices and reminders about the job in progress.
  • To manage the free first request, subscriptions and pay-per-use.
  • To measure professionals' reliability and improve the service.

Legal basis: performing the service you ask for (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR) and our legitimate interest in a reliable service and preventing abuse (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). For professionals, the first contact relies on legitimate interest and their public business contact details; during the call they can refuse any future contact.

We do not sell your data or use it for advertising.

Who we share it with

  • The other side of the job: the professional gets the business name, area, problem and contact phone; the business gets the professional's name and phone.
  • Service providers acting for us: Meta (WhatsApp Business), Supabase (database, EU - Ireland), Twilio (calls), Google (Maps), Anthropic (automatic sorting of the problem type, no payment data) and Stripe (payments).
  • Some of these providers may process data outside the EEA, under GDPR safeguards (standard contractual clauses or an adequacy decision).

How long we keep it

While you use Fixera and up to 24 months after your last job. Billing records are kept as long as tax law requires. The do-not-call list is kept as long as needed to honour it.

Your rights

You can ask for access, correction, deletion, objection, restriction and portability by writing to us on WhatsApp at +34 699 15 21 03. You can also complain to the Spanish Data Protection Agency (aepd.es) or your local authority.

How to delete your data

Send "DELETE MY DATA" on WhatsApp to +34 699 15 21 03 from the number you used with Fixera. We delete your data within 30 days, except what the law requires us to keep, and confirm on the same channel.
